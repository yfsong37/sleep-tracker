Your data

Stored in this browser. Export a backup, or move it to another device.

Backups drop into your Downloads folder — put that file in Google Drive (or any cloud folder) and re-import it anywhere to pick up where you left off.

Once connected, every change here is also saved to a single file in your Drive (My Drive → sleep-tracker-data.json), so opening this page on another device picks up your history automatically. Requires a one-time setup in Google Cloud Console — see the README.
